  • Why Us?

  • Athena’s belief system and heritage of seamless collaboration, innovation and diversification have enabled it to emerge as a single source for a wide range of talent management consulting services.


    • Our expertise expands to APAC, EMEA and the American markets


    • We are globally recognized by the Association of Executive Search and Leadership Consultants (AESC)


    • We have executed 450+ executive search assignments across industries and functional areas


    • We have delivered 250+ HR Consulting assignments on critical growth

  • We, at Athena, strive to revolutionize global businesses by executing holistic modern solutions to deliver direct impact, driven by innovation, conceptualization and ideation.


    Our unique and tailor-made approach in accelerating the growth of our clients emerges from our commitment towards entrepreneurship, novelty, ethics and professional excellence.


    Athena endorses a global network of over 350 consultants across 45 countries along with an array of scenario-specific services including India Entry, International Expansion and Transformation and Transition.

  • Athena began its journey with a mission to break past the conventional linear approach and lackluster solutioning, which was prevalent in the Indian executive search industry. Abiding by our core values to evolve and innovate, we have emerged as India’s premier independent boutique firm to be certified and recognized by AESC.

    Our vision, thus, lies in our devotedness to continue this journey with the same passion, vigor and discipline as we aspire to become one of the most prestigious and leading firms in the executive search industry worldwide.

    • Globally acclaimed business methodologies and practices delivered in a tailor-made model
    • Cloud-based platform ensuring complete transparency, real-time reporting, monitoring, and advanced analytics throughout the search lifecycle
    • International standards of corporate excellence, ethics, candidate care and client services
    • Committed to providing the most effective growth strategies covering a unique bouquet of services addressing some of the most critical talent management issues in distinct scenarios
    • Unrivalled global coverage with the capability to reach executive talent worldwide through our network of 350+ consultants across 45 countries
