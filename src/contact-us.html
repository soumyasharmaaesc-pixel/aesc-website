    FAQ

    Your questions answered

    How quickly will someone get back to me?

    +

    A partner or senior consultant responds personally, usually within one business day. Enquiries are never routed to an automated queue.

    What information helps us respond faster?

    +

    The role or challenge, the sector and the geography are enough to start. If you have a brief, attach it and we will come back with a considered point of view.

    Do you work outside India and the UAE?

    +

    Yes. We are based in Gurgaon and Dubai, and work through partners in 45 countries across APAC, EMEA and the Americas. See our offices.

    Is my enquiry confidential?

    +

    Entirely. Discretion is fundamental to leadership hiring, and nothing you share leaves Athena without your explicit consent.

    What can you actually help with?

    +

    Retained search for CEO, CXO and board roles, plus consulting: talent mapping, due diligence, market intelligence and compensation benchmarking. Search Services or Consulting & Advisory.

    Can we see proof before we engage?

    +

    Yes. We publish full mandates, including the brief, the reach and the appointment. Browse our case studies.

    How does AI feature in your process?

    +

    Athena is powered by TheHireHub.ai for sourcing and matching, with every shortlist assessed and defended by a partner. See how it works.

    I am a candidate, not a client. Where do I go?

    +

    Join our talent network and a consultant will reach out when a mandate fits your profile. Submit your resume or write to talent@aesc.co.in.
