    What We Do

    Our Consulting Offerings

    ◆

    Talent Mapping

    • Athena’s Talent Supply Chain Solution (ATSS) offers a proactive program to elevate talent acquisition operations.
    • The Talent Mapping process ensures clients are “Hire-Ready”, especially in growth and succession-planning scenarios.
    • ATSS maintains a pipeline for specific and niche talent as a virtual bench and talent topography.
    • Projects run on a real-time, highly intuitive cloud platform for complete transparency and monitoring.
    ◉

    Market Intelligence

    • As a strategic advisor, Athena delivers customized services that minimise the business risks of new market entry.
    • New-market ventures face challenges across competition, geography, talent dynamics, budget and feasibility.
    • Meticulous market intelligence is key to weighing strengths, weaknesses and opportunities on entry.
    • Our experience gives global clients actionable insight into feasibility, talent topographies, comparative landscapes and compensation benchmarks.
    ◇

    Persona Due Diligence

    • Athena’s unique Persona Due Diligence minimises business, cultural and reputational risks in transaction-based scenarios.
    • A comprehensive 360-degree target view covering human-capital / skill-gap evaluation and reputational-risk assessment.
    • We assess the ‘people’ potential of the target firm through extensive primary and secondary research.
    • In select cases we act as exclusive advisors to identify acquisition targets for M&A transactions.
    ★

    Compensation Benchmarking

    • Compensation and competitive benchmarking governed by real-time, accurate and targeted data.
    • Enables clients to make actionable decisions on their employee acquisition and retention blueprint.
    • Expertise in tailor-made comparator pools specific to each client’s business challenge.
    • A comprehensive, consultative analysis that delivers profound, actionable recommendations.
