    Persona Due Diligence

    The people risk in a deal, assessed before you sign

    Financial and legal due diligence catch most risks in a transaction. The softer, human risks that sink integrations are usually missed.

    Athena's Persona Due Diligence gives you a 360-degree view of the target's leadership, culture and reputational exposure, so you understand the people you are acquiring before the deal closes.

    How we help you

    A 360-degree view of the people you are buying

    1

    Leadership and skill-gap review

    We evaluate the target's key leaders and the capability gaps that will surface after close.

    2

    Culture and reputation assessment

    Extensive primary and secondary research surfaces cultural and reputational risk early.

    3

    Integration-ready findings

    You receive a clear read on retention risk and the leadership moves integration will require.

    Good to know

    Frequently asked questions

    When in a deal should Persona Due Diligence happen?

    +

    Ideally alongside commercial and financial diligence, before the transaction closes, so people risk informs the decision and the integration plan.

    What does the assessment cover?

    +

    Leadership capability, skill gaps, cultural fit and reputational risk across the target's key people, supported by primary and secondary research.

    How do we start, and how quickly will someone come back to us?

    +

    Tell us the role or challenge and a partner responds personally, usually within one business day. Enquiries are never routed to an automated queue. Talk to our partners to begin.

    Can we see examples of your work before we commit?

    +

    Yes. We publish real mandates end to end, including the brief, how far we searched and who was appointed. Browse our case studies.

    Who actually runs the work day to day?

    +

    A partner, throughout. There are no handoffs to junior researchers after the pitch, and no portal in between. That is the point of a boutique. See how we are different.

    Where do you operate?

    +

    We work from Gurgaon in India and Dubai in the UAE, and through partners in 45 countries across APAC, EMEA and the Americas. Find your nearest office.

    How does AI feature in your process?

    +

    Athena is powered by TheHireHub.ai for sourcing and matching, with every shortlist still assessed and defended by a partner. See how AI works in our process.

    Is our enquiry confidential?

    +

    Entirely. Discretion is fundamental to leadership hiring, and nothing you share leaves Athena without your explicit consent.
