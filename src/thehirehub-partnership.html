    That partner is TheHireHub.ai. Their platform is purpose-built for executive search, and we have integrated it into every Athena engagement. The result: our domain expertise, accelerated and sharpened by their technology.

    Case Studies

    Real engagements where TheHireHub.ai made a measurable difference.

    Manufacturing / Industrial

    CEO Search for a PE-Backed Industrial Group

    The Challenge

    A private equity firm needed a transformational CEO for a INR 2,000 Cr manufacturing group. The mandate required someone with P&L ownership at scale, board-level experience, and willingness to relocate to a Tier-2 city.

    How TheHireHub.ai Helped

    AI-powered sourcing identified 340+ potential candidates across India and the diaspora within 48 hours. The intelligent matching engine filtered down to 18 high-fit profiles based on leadership competencies, industry tenure, and relocation indicators.

    Results
    Shortlist delivered in 5 days (vs. typical 3 weeks)
    Candidate accepted within 4 weeks of mandate start
    3 backup candidates maintained in pipeline

    How TheHireHub.ai Helped

    Candidate still in role 18 months later
