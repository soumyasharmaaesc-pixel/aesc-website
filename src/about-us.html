    Good to know

    Frequently asked questions

    What does Athena Executive Search & Consulting do?

    +

    Athena is a boutique, partner-led executive search and leadership consulting firm. We help organisations hire and develop CEOs, board members and senior leaders, and we advise on leadership assessment, succession and organisation design.

    How is Athena different from a traditional recruitment agency?

    +

    Every mandate is led by a partner, not passed to junior researchers. We combine scientific assessment, deep market mapping and a tailor-made process, focusing on a small number of retained searches so each client receives senior attention and measurable results.

    Which industries and functions does Athena cover?

    +

    We run C-suite, board and functional leadership searches across sectors including technology, financial services, consumer, industrial, healthcare and professional services, spanning roles from CEO and CFO to CHRO, CTO and beyond.

    Where does Athena operate?

    +

    Athena delivers executive search and leadership advisory across APAC, EMEA and the Americas, supported by our global partnership with Penrhyn International and our technology partner TheHireHub.ai.

    How do I engage Athena for a leadership search?

    +

    Share your requirement through our contact page or email info@aesc.co.in. A partner will respond personally to understand the role, the context and the outcome you need before proposing a tailored search approach.
