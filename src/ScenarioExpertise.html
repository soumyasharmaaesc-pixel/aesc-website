    A tailored approach for every corporate scenario

    Our scenario expertise captures the precise needs of each client to administer an intense, tailor-made approach — curated for seamless growth in any corporate context.

    From India Entry and International Expansion to turnaround, transformation and succession, we map the leadership required for the road ahead.

    Scenario expertise

    Built for the moments that change a business

    Three scenarios where leadership decisions carry the most risk, and where a partner-led approach makes the difference.

    01

    India Entry

    A cornerstone for international companies looking to expand their operations in India.

    • Market research and feasibility, with a pragmatic read on strengths, weaknesses and opportunities for entering the Indian market.
    • Strategy planning designed to minimise business risk from day one.
    • Competitive analysis that turns the competitor landscape into actionable insight for better business planning.
    Explore India Entry →
    02

    International Expansion

    Global expansion expertise across Asia Pacific, EMEA and the Americas.

    • Support as a strategic partner on diverse mandates, from facilitating M&A transactions to developing alliances in targeted industry segments.
    • Executive search and business advisory delivered alongside the transaction, not after it.
    • Partners in 45 important locations across APAC, EMEA and the Americas, giving deep understanding of regional markets.
    Explore International Expansion →
    03

    Transformation and Transition

    Directing accurate, strategic outcomes through a turnaround by deploying managerial expertise.

    • A complete range of transition services: project planning, organisation design, determination of key functions and roles, staff resource mapping, business process modelling and integration management.
    • Extensive experience handling layoffs and organisational restructuring with a comprehensive, considered approach.
    • Competent interim management during the turnaround, plus outplacement services where needed.

    How we help you

    Frequently asked questions

    Straight answers on how Athena works across India entry, international expansion and transformation mandates.

    What are the services that Athena offers in scenarios of India entry?

    +

    Athena has partnered its clients in scenarios of new market entry through services spanning executive and interim search, location analysis, talent topography, competitive benchmarking, and market research.

    How does Athena support its clients in scenarios of international expansion?

    +

    We play a pivotal role as the strategic partner on diverse mandates, including facilitating M&A transactions, developing strategic alliances within targeted industry segments, and providing executive search and business advisory support.

    How does Athena help its clients in scenarios of transition and transformation?

    +

    Our services address the challenges at the centre of a turnaround: interim management, organisation design, determination of key functions and roles, mapping of staff resources, business process modelling and integration management.

    Which markets does Athena cover for scenario-based mandates?

    +

    We operate from Gurgaon in India and Dubai in the UAE, and work through partners in 45 locations across APAC, EMEA and the Americas, giving us direct understanding of regional talent markets.

    How long does a scenario expertise engagement take?

    +

    It depends on the scenario. A single leadership hire for a market entry typically runs eight to twelve weeks, while a full transformation or India entry programme is scoped in phases against your business milestones.

    Does Athena provide interim leadership as well as permanent hires?

    +

    Yes. Interim management is a core part of our transformation and transition work, bringing in competent leadership during a turnaround, alongside outplacement support where restructuring is involved.

    What makes a scenario-based search different from a standard executive search?

    +

    The brief is built around a business event rather than a job title. We map the leadership a market entry, expansion or turnaround actually requires, then assess candidates for judgement in that specific context.
