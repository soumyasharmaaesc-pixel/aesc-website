    India Entry

    • Athena serves as a cornerstone for international companies looking to expand their operations in India

    • Our India Entry Advisory services are also steered by Market Research & Feasibility, and a pragmatic approach to weigh the strengths, weaknesses and opportunities for clients entering the Indian market to further plan strategies that minimize business risks

    • We also incorporate a Competitive Analysis which is a paramount service that assists our clients to gather actionable insights on the competitor landscape resulting in improved business planning

    International Expansion

    • Athena's international business expertise provides its clients seeking global expansion across Asia Pacific, EMEA, and the Americas

    • We provide invaluable support and guidance as a strategic partner with diverse mandates such as facilitating M&A transactions, developing strategic alliances within targeted industry segments, and providing executive search and business advisory

    • Through our partners in 45 important locations across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas, Athena has deep experience and understanding of regional markets

    Transformation and Transition

    • A turnaround period is a key facet of Athena’s transformation and transition services which aims at directing accurate and strategic outcomes through deploying managerial expertise

    • We provide a complete range of services indispensable to the transition process such as project planning, organization design, determination of key functions and roles, staff resource mapping, business process modelling and integration management, to name a few

    • Athena has extensive expertise of handling layoffs and organisational restructuring where we take a comprehensive approach to bringing in competent interim management if needed during the turnaround process, as well as providing outplacement services
