    Our Team Members

    Bhavishya Sharma
    Founder & Managing Director

    Bhavishya is a tremendously passionate and driven personality, under whose leadership Athena has accelerated to become one of the world’s premier Executive Search and Leadership Advisory organizations. It is through his resilience and perseverance that Athena has been established as the first ever independent retained search firm to be accredited as an AESC firm.

    With over two decades of rich experience and a dedicated career in the executive search industry, Bhavishya is a trusted advisor of clients internationally on all aspects of identification, assessment and recruitment for leadership and board roles at both Board and C-Suite levels. Bhavishya’s leading expertise in consulting has enabled him to advise a large portfolio of clients on strategic talent management challenges like Market Entry Advisory, HR Due-Diligence, Board Advisory, Leadership Assessment and Compensation Benchmarking with complete finesse. He is also an exclusive M&A advisor to global organizations in the Technology and Professional Services industry.

    Bhavishya has earned an engineering degree from the prestigious Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi, and he splendidly delivers corporate solutions, stirred by an unquenchable thirst for knowledge and a vigour for the industry.
